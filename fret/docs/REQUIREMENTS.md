# Requirements given to FRET

Generated by `python -m secure_agent_attach doc` from the files in `requirements/`. Do not edit.

## How to read

Every requirement is one sentence in FRETish, the structured English of FRET. FRET's parser turns the sentence into a temporal-logic formula, which is then checked by NuSMV and Kind2. A sentence names a component (the part of the system that must behave), says when the requirement holds and what the component must satisfy. Here every requirement holds at every step (`always`, or `never` for a forbidden event). The conditions are made of signals; a signal is either chosen by the environment (Input) or decided by the system (Output).

Example, requirement GATE-03:

- Sentence: `Gate shall always satisfy ((!g_ticket_found \| !g_ticket_bound \| g_ticket_expired) => (g_permission_denied & !g_validate_called & !g_resources_called & !g_execute_called))`
- Meaning: If the ticket is missing, belongs to another turn or has expired, the call is denied and no tool code is called.

`{NAME}` in a sentence is a shorthand for a longer condition. The expressions are in the macro table at the end. `SEC` marks a security requirement, `FUN` a statement of what the code does. "Original" tells whether the unmodified Secure_Agent already satisfies the requirement.

## Turn state machine

Eight requirements on the turn loop, checked with NuSMV on a model of `process_turn` and on end-to-end scenarios.

| ID | Title | FRETish | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| SR-01 | Gate conformance | `Core shall never satisfy exec_unauthorized` | A tool executes only with a valid ticket, inside the grants, within the call budget. |
| SR-02 | Least privilege | `Core shall always satisfy (ticket_exists => grants_within_plan)` | Grants never exceed what the plan requested. The verifier prompt asks for it. |
| SR-03 | Policy bound | `Core shall always satisfy (ticket_exists => grants_within_policy)` | Grants never exceed agent_permissions and user_permissions, whatever the verifier says. |
| SR-04 | No ticket after the turn | `Core shall always satisfy (turn_over => !ticket_exists)` | Every way out of the turn revokes the ticket. |
| SR-05 | Expired ticket is not a dead end | `when blocked_start Core shall at the next timepoint satisfy (stage_authorize \| turn_over)` | A deterministic denial must not be retried like a transient error. After a blocked start the next iteration re-authorizes or the turn ends. |
| SR-06 | A denied turn does not end silently | `Core shall always satisfy ((turn_over & denial_seen) => result_has_reason)` | If a call was denied or blocked, the turn result carries a message. Weak form: any non-empty message counts. |
| SR-07 | Operator prompt provenance | `Core shall never satisfy managed_untrusted` | managed entries come only from the operator configuration, never from a restored history. |
| SR-08 | Bounded loop | `Core shall always satisfy (steps <= max_steps)` | The turn loop never exceeds its limit. |

## Tool-call monitor

The model is treated as an attacker. Each requirement states a line that must hold whatever the model does.

| ID | Attack | Title | FRETish | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CM-01 | privilege escalation | No unauthorized execution | `Monitor shall never satisfy exec_unauthorized` | A tool runs only with a valid ticket, inside the grants and inside the policy, whatever the model asks for. |
| CM-02 | privilege escalation | Least privilege | `Monitor shall never satisfy exec_outside_plan` | A verifier that adds a tool the plan never asked for must not widen what can run (privilege escalation through the verifier). |
| CM-03 | exfiltration chain | No send after a sensitive read | `after exec_sens Monitor shall always satisfy !exec_send` | Each tool is allowed on its own, but the chain read-then-send leaks. After a sensitive read in the session no external send executes, however the model orders its calls. |
| CM-04 | resource exhaustion | Model-call budget | `Monitor shall never satisfy llm_over_cap` | A turn cannot make the system spend without limit: no model call is made once the budget of the turn is used up. |
| CM-05 | false completion | No completion without evidence | `Monitor shall never satisfy completed_no_evidence` | A turn whose plan needed a tool is not completed unless a tool call succeeded. |

## Realizability of the monitor
